Introduction

Welcome to Convofy, an AI-based personalized telemedicine platform. At Convofy, we are committed to ensuring the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your data when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We collect information provided by you during registration, including your name, contact details, and other necessary information. Your health records, medical history, and relevant details are collected to facilitate personalized telemedicine consultations.

b. Usage Information

We gather data about your interactions with the platform, such as log files, device information, and usage patterns, to enhance our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

a. Personalized Healthcare

Your health records and information are utilized to provide personalized telemedicine consultations and medical advice.

b. Improvement of Services

We analyze user data to enhance our platform's functionality, usability, and overall user experience.

3. Data Security

a. Secure Storage

All data, including health records and credentials, is stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

b. Limited Access

Only authorized personnel have access to your data, and they are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Sharing

a. Non-Disclosure

Convofy does not share your personal information, health records, or any other data with third-party platforms or services.

b. Legal Compliance

We may disclose information if required by law, in response to legal requests, or to protect our rights and interests.

5. Consent and Control

a. User Control

You have the right to access, modify, or delete your personal information. You can manage your preferences through your account settings.

b. Opt-out

You may choose to opt-out of certain data collection and communications by adjusting your preferences.

6. Changes to Privacy Policy

a. Notification

Any changes to our Privacy Policy will be communicated through the website or via email. Continued use of Convofy implies acceptance of these changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@convofy.health.

By using Convofy, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. Thank you for trusting us with your healthcare needs.
